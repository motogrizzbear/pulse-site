Privacy

Pulse Privacy Policy

Pulse is designed so your workout and bladder-diary data stays on your devices unless you explicitly choose to export it or use an Apple system integration described below.

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

Pulse is a local-first iPhone and Apple Watch app. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated servers.

Data stored on your devices

Workout plans, workout history, bladder voiding records (including stream duration, urge level, leakage severity, and optional activity notes), fluid intake logs, goals, reminder settings, and app preferences are stored locally on your devices using Apple system frameworks. Pulse does not transmit this information to the developer or to third parties.

The Apple Watch app uses Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework to receive workout plans from the paired iPhone and return completed workout sessions and bladder logs. This transfer occurs through Apple’s device-to-device services and is not sent to a Pulse-operated server.

Apple Health

If you enable Health sync, Pulse can write completed workouts to Apple Health. Pulse does not read your existing Health data. Health access is optional and controlled through Apple’s permission settings.

Notifications

Workout reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Pulse does not use a remote notification server.

Exporting and deleting data

History and bladder diary exports only occur when you request them and choose where to share the resulting CSV files. Once shared, the destination you select handles that copy under its own privacy practices.

You can delete individual records or clear all workout history and bladder logs inside Pulse. Deleting Pulse removes its locally stored app data. Workouts previously written to Apple Health remain under your control in the Health app.

Public support website

This website provides product information, support documentation, and this privacy policy. It is separate from the Pulse app and does not receive your workout history, bladder diary, Health data, or other locally stored app records.

The Pulse support site does not intentionally add analytics, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, or a user account system. If you choose a light or dark appearance override, that preference is stored locally in your browser and is not sent to Pulse. This site is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web requests and security logs, such as IP addresses, under its own terms and privacy practices.

Do not include sensitive medical or bladder-diary information in public support channels. Use the private support contact listed on Pulse’s App Store page when you need to contact the developer.

Medical information

Pulse provides exercise guidance and personal voiding-habit tracking. It is not intended to provide medical advice, diagnose a medical condition, or replace care from a physician or pelvic-health professional.

Changes

If Pulse’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the related app update is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the private developer contact published on Pulse’s public support page or App Store listing.
